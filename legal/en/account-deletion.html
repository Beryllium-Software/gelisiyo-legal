Account and data deletion

Gelişiyo · Effective: 13 November 2026

Data controller: Burcu Öztürk Beryllium Software, Kızılırmak Mah. Dumlupınar Bulvarı No:9A YDA Center Daire 158, Çankaya/Ankara 06530, Türkiye · info@berylliumsoftware.com

You can request deletion of your Gelişiyo account and the personal data stored with it. The app does not currently offer one-tap automatic deletion; deletion is carried out after a request sent through the channels below.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app, go to Settings → Legal & data → Account and data requests and tap “Email a request”; or
  2. Email info@berylliumsoftware.com from the address registered in the app. Suggested subject: “Gelişiyo — Account deletion request”. Include your registered email address and state that you want the account deleted.

For security we verify the request via your registered email. We may ask for additional confirmation.

Timeline

After we verify your request, we will complete it within 30 days, in line with Article 13 of Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK). We will email you when it is done. If the period is extended in exceptional cases allowed by that law, we will inform you.

What is deleted

  • Your account and authentication record (email, name, profile details, avatar).
  • Notification preferences and device push token.
  • Chat history and AI memory records associated with you.
  • If no other active family member remains: family and child records and related growth, vaccination, notes, media (“firsts” photos and videos) and similar content.

What may be retained

  • If other active family members remain, child and family records stay with them. Your membership and personal account are deleted; we will clarify the status of remaining data when handling the request.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, for the applicable period.
  • Limited technical records for security and abuse prevention, for a proportionate period.
  • Short-lived remnants in backups, which are deleted or anonymised at the end of the backup cycle.
  • Anonymised statistics and general items in the public content library (not tied to your personal account) are unaffected.

See also the Privacy Policy. For access or correction requests, use the same email address.
