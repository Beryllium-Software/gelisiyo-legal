1. Scope

This policy explains how we process personal data when you use the Gelişiyo mobile app. The app helps parents track child development; it is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or emergency care.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email, authentication data (Supabase Auth), first and last name, date of birth, profile gender, language and theme preferences.
  • Family and child: child name, birth date and gender you enter; family membership and roles; optional child photo.
  • App content: notes, tracker entries (sleep, feeding, growth measurements, vaccines, teeth, firsts, words, safety, development surveys, etc.), favorites.
  • Feed: viewing published articles/surveys/media; technical records of which items were seen.
  • Photos and media: images and videos you upload (for example profile photos and “firsts”).
  • AI chat: your messages and the child/family context used to generate replies (see section 5).
  • Notifications: device push token (if you allow); inbox records.
  • Technical: records needed for security and abuse prevention.

3. Purposes

  • Account, authentication, and providing the service
  • Notes, tracking, surveys, and content features
  • Personalized feed and age-appropriate content
  • AI-assisted chat (with disclaimers and limits)
  • Push notifications (with consent)
  • Security, troubleshooting, and legal compliance

4. Legal bases

We rely on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent (e.g. push notifications, optional features), and legal obligations under applicable law, including Turkey’s KVKK. Health-related notes you enter may be sensitive; you provide them only to use the service.

5. Artificial intelligence and OpenAI

The optional in-app chat may send your messages and the selected child/family context (for example age, development and health-related notes you entered) to the third-party AI provider OpenAI in order to generate a reply. This is not done for advertising. AI outputs are not medical advice or a diagnosis and may be wrong or incomplete; do not rely on them alone for critical decisions. OpenAI’s servers may be located outside Turkey; transfers are made over HTTPS with contractual safeguards.

6. Children’s data

Child data is entered by parents or legal guardians. The app is not directed at children under 13 opening their own accounts; Gelişiyo is for parents aged 18 and over. Processing follows parent-account and family-sharing rules.

7. Processors and transfers

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage (EU, eu-west-1)
  • Expo / Apple / Google — distribution, push infrastructure, optional Google sign-in
  • OpenAI — AI chat processing (see section 5)

Servers may be in Turkey, the EU, or other regions with appropriate safeguards.

8. Retention

Data is kept while your account is active and as required by law. Deletion or erasure requests are handled within the time limits under KVKK; short-lived backups may remain. See Account and data deletion.

9. Your rights and account deletion

Contact info@berylliumsoftware.com for access, correction, deletion, or complaints. In the app: Settings → Legal & data → Account and data requests. Step-by-step: Account and data deletion.

10. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit (HTTPS), and least-privilege practices. No system is 100% secure.

11. Changes

We may update this page; the effective date will change. Material updates may be announced in-app or by email.
